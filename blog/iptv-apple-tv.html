IPTV on Apple TV: Best Apps & Complete Setup Guide (2025)

6 min read July 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

The Apple TV 4K is one of the best streaming devices available, but its tvOS ecosystem has fewer IPTV app options than Android TV. Fortunately, several high-quality apps are available on the App Store — and they work excellently with BestIPTVToday.

Best IPTV Apps for Apple TV

1. GSE Smart IPTV

The top choice for Apple TV IPTV. Supports M3U, Xtream Codes API, and EPG. Available on the App Store for Apple TV, iPhone, and iPad — purchase once, use on all Apple devices.

  • Full Xtream Codes support (Live TV, VOD, Series)
  • Advanced EPG with multi-source support
  • Syncs across iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV
  • Price: Free with optional paid features

2. IPTV Smarters Pro

Available on the Apple TV App Store. Supports Xtream Codes and M3U — the same interface as the iOS version. A solid all-round choice.

3. Infuse 7

Primarily a video player, but Infuse can open M3U streams directly. Best for playing individual VOD streams rather than full channel lists.

Setting Up GSE Smart IPTV on Apple TV

  1. Open the App Store on your Apple TV
  2. Search for "GSE Smart IPTV" and install
  3. Open GSE Smart IPTV
  4. Navigate to Remote Playlists
  5. Tap the + icon → Xtream Codes API
  6. Enter your BestIPTVToday Server URL, Username, and Password
  7. Tap Add — all channels, VOD, and EPG load automatically

Apple TV tip: Use your iPhone or iPad to set up GSE Smart IPTV first, then it syncs to your Apple TV automatically via iCloud. Much easier than typing long URLs with the Apple TV remote.

Alternative: AirPlay from iPhone/iPad

If you prefer using an IPTV app on your iPhone or iPad that is not available on Apple TV, you can AirPlay the content to your Apple TV:

  1. Open your IPTV app on iPhone/iPad (IPTV Smarters, GSE, etc.)
  2. Start playing a channel
  3. Tap the AirPlay icon (rectangle with triangle) in the video player
  4. Select your Apple TV from the list
  5. The stream immediately appears on your TV — full quality
