How to Add M3U Playlist to Any IPTV App (2025 Guide)

6 min read April 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

Got your M3U URL from your IPTV provider? This guide shows you exactly how to add it to every major app — TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, VLC, Smart IPTV, SS IPTV, GSE Smart IPTV, and more.

TiviMate (Android TV / Firestick)

  1. Open TiviMate → tap the screen or press OK on your remote
  2. Select Add Playlist
  3. Choose M3U Playlist
  4. Paste your M3U URL in the text field
  5. Give it a name (e.g. "BestIPTVToday")
  6. Tap Next → add an EPG URL if available → tap Add

IPTV Smarters Pro (Android, iOS, Windows)

  1. Open IPTV Smarters Pro
  2. Tap Add User on the home screen
  3. Select Load Your Playlist / Stream / M3U URL
  4. Enter a nickname and paste your M3U URL
  5. Tap Add User

VLC Media Player (Windows, Mac, Linux, Android, iOS)

  1. Open VLC
  2. Go to Media → Open Network Stream (or press Ctrl+N on PC)
  3. Paste your M3U URL
  4. Click Play
  5. VLC will load the playlist — use the playlist view to browse channels

Smart IPTV (Samsung & LG Smart TVs)

  1. Install Smart IPTV from your TV's app store
  2. Note the MAC address shown in the app
  3. Visit the Smart IPTV website on your computer
  4. Enter your TV's MAC address and paste your M3U URL
  5. Click Send — reopen the app on your TV

SS IPTV (Samsung Smart TVs)

  1. Install SS IPTV from Samsung App Store
  2. Open the app and go to Settings → Playlist → Add External Playlist
  3. Enter a name and paste your M3U URL
  4. Tap Save

GSE Smart IPTV (iOS / Android)

  1. Open GSE Smart IPTV
  2. Tap the + icon → Remote Playlist M3U
  3. Enter a playlist name and your M3U URL
  4. Tap Add

Tip: Always copy-paste your M3U URL rather than typing it manually. A single wrong character will prevent the playlist from loading.
