TiviMate Setup Guide 2025: Complete Step-by-Step Tutorial

8 min read January 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

TiviMate is the gold standard IPTV player for Android TV devices. Whether you are setting it up for the first time or migrating to a new device, this 2025 guide walks you through every step — from installation to advanced configuration.

Step 1 — Install TiviMate

TiviMate is available on the Google Play Store for Android TV devices (NVIDIA Shield, Chromecast with Google TV, Mi Box). On Amazon Firestick, you need to sideload it via the Downloader app.

On Android TV / Google TV

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your device
  2. Search for "TiviMate IPTV Player"
  3. Click Install and wait for completion
  4. Launch TiviMate from your app list

On Amazon Firestick

  1. Go to Settings → My Fire TV → Developer Options
  2. Enable Apps from Unknown Sources
  3. Install the Downloader app from the Amazon Store
  4. Open Downloader, enter the TiviMate APK URL and install

Pro tip: Always download TiviMate from the official source only. Avoid third-party APK sites that may bundle modified versions.

Step 2 — Add Your IPTV Playlist

TiviMate supports two connection methods. Xtream Codes is recommended for BestIPTVToday subscribers as it enables EPG, Catch-Up TV, and VOD automatically.

Method A — Xtream Codes (Recommended)

  1. Open TiviMate and tap "Add Playlist"
  2. Select "Xtream Codes API"
  3. Enter the Server URL, Username, and Password from your BestIPTVToday confirmation email
  4. Tap Next — TiviMate will load all channels and VOD content
  5. Add your EPG URL on the next screen for the TV guide

Method B — M3U Playlist URL

  1. Tap "Add Playlist" → "M3U Playlist"
  2. Paste your M3U URL in the URL field
  3. Name the playlist and tap Add

Pro Tips for TiviMate 2025

  • Favourite Groups: Long-press a channel group and pin it to the top of your list
  • Multi-View (Premium): Watch up to 4 channels simultaneously — great for sports
  • Parental Lock: Set a PIN on adult channel groups for family safety
  • Catch-Up TV: Enable in Settings → Catch-Up to replay up to 7 days of programming
  • LAN Adapter: For Firestick, a USB-to-ethernet adapter dramatically reduces buffering
  • Export Settings: Back up your TiviMate config to a file before resetting your device
