Xtream IPTV Player Guide: Best Apps & Setup in 2025

7 min read April 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

Not all IPTV apps support Xtream Codes equally well. Some provide basic channel lists while others unlock the full VOD library, EPG, Catch-Up TV, and series sections. This guide ranks the top Xtream Codes IPTV players and shows you how to set up each one.

What Makes a Great Xtream Codes Player?

  • Loads all three Xtream sections: Live TV, VOD Movies, TV Series
  • Displays the EPG (electronic programme guide) automatically
  • Supports Catch-Up TV from the Xtream Codes API
  • Shows your account expiry date
  • Optimised for your device type (remote control, touchscreen, mouse)

Top 5 Xtream Codes IPTV Players

1. TiviMate — Best for Android TV

TiviMate is the gold standard for Android TV and Firestick. It fully supports all three Xtream Codes sections, has an exceptional EPG, and the Premium version unlocks Catch-Up and multi-view. Remote-control navigation is flawless.

2. IPTV Smarters Pro — Best Multi-Platform

Available on Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac. The "Xtream Codes API" login mode loads live TV, VOD, and series in a Netflix-style interface. Perfect for households with mixed devices.

3. GSE Smart IPTV — Best for iOS

The top choice for iPhone and iPad users. Full Xtream Codes support including VOD, series, and EPG. Also available on Apple TV.

4. Televizo — Best Free Option

Completely free, no Premium tier needed. Supports Xtream Codes with full live TV, VOD, EPG, and Catch-Up. Great card-based UI for Android TV.

5. Perfect Player — Best Lightweight Option

Minimal resource usage. Ideal for older Android boxes. Supports Xtream Codes for live channels and EPG. VOD/series support is more limited.
