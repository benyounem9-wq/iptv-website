TiviMate Premium APK Guide: The Right Way to Get Premium (2025)

5 min read June 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

When searching for "TiviMate Premium APK", you will find hundreds of unofficial sites offering "cracked" or "modded" APKs. This guide explains the safest and most reliable way to get TiviMate Premium — and why unofficial APKs are risky.

What is TiviMate Premium?

TiviMate Premium is an in-app purchase that unlocks advanced features including:

  • Multi-View: Watch up to 4 channels simultaneously
  • Catch-Up TV: Replay up to 7 days of past broadcasts
  • Recording: Record live TV to external storage
  • Multiple playlists: Add unlimited IPTV providers
  • Multiple profiles: Separate setups for family members

The cost: approximately $4.99/year or $14.99 lifetime — one of the best value app upgrades available.

How to Get TiviMate Premium Legitimately

On Android TV / Google TV

  1. Install TiviMate free from the Google Play Store
  2. Open TiviMate → go to Settings (gear icon)
  3. Scroll down to Premium
  4. Tap Activate Premium
  5. Select annual or lifetime plan
  6. Pay via your Google Play account — activates instantly on all devices sharing the same Google account

On Amazon Firestick (Sideloaded TiviMate)

  1. TiviMate on Firestick cannot use Google Play for purchases
  2. Open TiviMate → Settings → Premium → Activate via Google Account
  3. A browser link is shown — open it on your phone or PC while logged into the same Google account
  4. Complete the Google Play purchase on your phone
  5. Restart TiviMate on Firestick — Premium activates automatically

Warning — Unofficial APKs: Modded TiviMate APKs from third-party sites often contain malware, crash unpredictably, and can steal your IPTV credentials. At $5/year, the official Premium is not worth the risk of using a cracked version.

TiviMate Free APK for Firestick (Official Method)

To install the free version of TiviMate on Firestick (which is not on the Amazon Store), you do need to sideload the APK:

  1. Install Downloader from the Amazon App Store
  2. Open Downloader and enter the TiviMate APK URL from the official TiviMate website (tivimate.com)
  3. Download and install — the free version works fully, with Premium features unlocked via Google Play purchase
