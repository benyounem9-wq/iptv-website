TiviMate Premium Features: Is the Upgrade Worth It in 2025?

6 min read April 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

TiviMate is free to download but locks its most powerful features behind a Premium subscription. At around $5/year or $15 lifetime, it is one of the best value upgrades in the streaming world. Here is every Premium feature explained, so you can decide if it suits your needs.

Multi-View: Watch 4 Channels At Once

Multi-View is the flagship Premium feature. You can display up to 4 channels simultaneously on the same screen — perfect for sports fans who want to watch multiple games at once. You can customise the split layout (2x2 grid, 1 large + 3 small, etc.).

Catch-Up TV: Never Miss a Show

Catch-Up lets you go back in time and watch programmes that already aired — up to 7 days in the past. Combined with BestIPTVToday's Catch-Up support, this is the single most compelling reason to go Premium. It effectively works like a free DVR for your entire channel lineup.

Recording Feature

Premium subscribers can record live TV to an external USB drive or network storage. Set recordings manually or schedule them from the EPG. Perfect for shows you want to watch offline or archive permanently.

Multiple Playlists

Free TiviMate only supports one M3U playlist. Premium removes this limit — useful if you have a primary subscription plus a backup provider, or separate playlists for work and home.

Our verdict: At $5/year, TiviMate Premium is essentially free. If you use BestIPTVToday with Catch-Up TV support, Premium pays for itself on day one. Highly recommended.

How to Buy TiviMate Premium

  1. Open TiviMate on your Android TV or Firestick
  2. Go to Settings → Premium
  3. Select your plan (annual or lifetime)
  4. Purchase via Google Play — applies instantly across all devices logged in with the same Google account
