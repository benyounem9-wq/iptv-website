Best IPTV Player for Android TV in 2025: Top 6 Apps Ranked

8 min read March 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

Android TV powers millions of smart TVs, NVIDIA Shields, Chromecast with Google TV units, and Mi Box devices. Choosing the right IPTV player makes the difference between a frustrating and an effortless streaming experience. We tested every major app in 2025 — here are the results.

🥇 #1 — TiviMate IPTV Player

Best for: Users who want the richest feature set and the most polished TV-remote-driven interface.

  • Exceptional EPG with 7-day programme guide
  • Multi-view (watch 4 channels simultaneously)
  • Catch-Up TV, recording, multiple playlists (Premium)
  • Price: Free / ~$5/year Premium

🥈 #2 — Televizo

Best for: Users who want a free, modern card-based UI without a Premium tier.

  • Unlimited playlists for free
  • Clean card layout with channel logos
  • Good EPG and VOD support
  • Price: 100% Free

🥉 #3 — IPTV Smarters Pro

Best for: Users who also want the same app on their phone or laptop.

  • Available on Android, iOS, Windows, and Mac
  • Xtream Codes + M3U + Stalker Portal support
  • Movie-style poster view for VOD
  • Price: Free (Android) / ~$5 (iOS)

#4 — GSE Smart IPTV

Excellent cross-platform support including Apple TV. Supports advanced EPG merging from multiple sources.

#5 — Perfect Player

Lightweight and fast. Great for Android TV boxes with limited resources. Free with all essential features.

#6 — IBO Player Pro

Designed for the MAG-box style experience. Ideal if you prefer a classic satellite-TV-style interface.
