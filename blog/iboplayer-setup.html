IBO Player Setup Guide: Install & Configure in 2025

6 min read May 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

IBO Player Pro is a popular IPTV app for Android TV and Amazon Firestick that mimics the classic MAG box interface. It supports Xtream Codes, M3U playlists, EPG, and VOD — and is available directly from the Amazon App Store without sideloading.

How to Install IBO Player Pro

On Amazon Firestick

  1. Open the Amazon App Store on your Firestick
  2. Search for "IBO Player Pro"
  3. Click Download — no sideloading required
  4. Open IBO Player Pro from your apps list

On Android TV / Google TV

  1. Open the Google Play Store
  2. Search for "IBO Player Pro"
  3. Install and launch the app

Add Your IPTV Service

Via Xtream Codes

  1. Open IBO Player Pro and tap Add Playlist
  2. Select Xtream Codes
  3. Enter your Server URL, Username, and Password
  4. Tap Confirm — IBO Player will import all content

Via M3U URL

  1. Tap Add Playlist → M3U / URL
  2. Enter the playlist name and paste your M3U URL
  3. Tap Confirm

IBO Player Key Features

  • MAG-style UI: Familiar classic IPTV interface for satellite TV users
  • EPG Guide: Electronic programme guide with 7-day view
  • VOD & Series: Full video-on-demand library access via Xtream Codes
  • Multiple providers: Add and switch between IPTV services
  • Parental control: PIN-lock adult content
  • Catch-Up TV: Replay past broadcasts (provider-dependent)

Tip: IBO Player Pro's main advantage is being available on the Amazon App Store without sideloading — ideal for users who prefer to keep their Firestick setup straightforward.
