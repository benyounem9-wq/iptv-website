IPTV on iPhone & iPad: Best Apps & Setup Guide (2025)

6 min read August 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

iOS devices make excellent portable IPTV players. The App Store has several high-quality IPTV apps that support M3U playlists, Xtream Codes, and EPG — allowing you to watch your subscription on iPhone and iPad anywhere with a Wi-Fi or 5G connection.

Best IPTV Apps for iPhone & iPad (2025)

1. IPTV Smarters Pro

The most popular IPTV app on iOS. Available on the App Store (~$4.99), it supports Xtream Codes API, M3U playlists, and EPG. The interface is clean and works well on both iPhone and iPad.

  • Xtream Codes, M3U, and Stalker Portal support
  • VOD with Netflix-style poster view
  • Parental control
  • Multiple provider profiles

2. GSE Smart IPTV

A powerful and flexible IPTV player. Supports all connection types, advanced EPG from multiple sources, and AirPlay to stream to Apple TV. Available free with premium features as in-app purchase.

3. Flex IPTV

Available free on the App Store. Supports M3U and Xtream Codes with a simple, clean interface. Good EPG support. A solid free alternative to IPTV Smarters.

4. OTT Navigator (TestFlight)

Available via TestFlight (Apple's beta testing platform). Full Xtream Codes support with a highly polished interface. Free but requires TestFlight invitation link from the developer.

Setup IPTV Smarters Pro on iPhone

  1. Download IPTV Smarters Pro from the App Store (~$4.99)
  2. Open the app → tap Add User
  3. Select Xtream Codes API
  4. Enter a nickname for your provider
  5. Enter the Server URL, Username, and Password from your BestIPTVToday email
  6. Tap Add User — all channels, VOD, and EPG load in seconds

AirPlay tip: Watching IPTV on iPhone? Tap the AirPlay icon in the video player to cast directly to your Apple TV or AirPlay-compatible Smart TV without any setup.
