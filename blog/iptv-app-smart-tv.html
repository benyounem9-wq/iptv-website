Best IPTV App for Smart TV in 2025: Samsung, LG & More

7 min read April 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

Smart TVs from Samsung, LG, Sony, and Hisense have their own app ecosystems — they cannot run Android apps directly. But several excellent IPTV apps are available for these platforms. Here is what works on each TV brand in 2025.

Samsung Smart TV IPTV Apps

Samsung uses the Tizen OS. Available IPTV apps from Samsung's App Store:

  • Smart IPTV — One-time €5.49 activation, excellent M3U support, supports EPG
  • SS IPTV — Free, great channel management, supports M3U and Xtream Codes
  • NET IPTV — Free, clean interface, M3U URL and Xtream Codes supported

LG Smart TV IPTV Apps

LG uses webOS. Available options:

  • Smart IPTV — Same app as Samsung version; webOS supported
  • IPTV Smarters LG — Available for some LG models via the LG Content Store
  • SS IPTV — Available on LG webOS app store

Sony & Hisense (Android TV Models)

Sony and Hisense Smart TVs often run Android TV or Google TV, giving access to the full Google Play Store. This means you can use:

  • TiviMate — Best option if your Sony/Hisense runs Android TV
  • IPTV Smarters Pro — Full Google Play version
  • Televizo — Free, modern interface

How to Set Up IPTV on Samsung Smart TV (SS IPTV)

  1. Search for SS IPTV in your Samsung Smart TV app store
  2. Install and open SS IPTV
  3. Go to Settings → Playlist → Add Playlist
  4. Select Remote (URL) and paste your M3U URL
  5. Save and return to the main screen — your channels will load
