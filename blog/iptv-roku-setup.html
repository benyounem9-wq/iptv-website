IPTV on Roku: Best Methods & Setup Guide (2025)

6 min read June 2025 BestIPTVToday Team

Roku's closed ecosystem makes IPTV slightly more challenging than on Android TV or Firestick — but it is absolutely possible. There are three main methods, ranging from the simplest (screen mirroring) to the most functional (private Roku channels).

Method 1 — Screen Mirroring (Easiest)

The simplest way to watch IPTV on Roku requires no special setup on the Roku itself:

  1. Install an IPTV app on your Android phone or tablet (IPTV Smarters Pro or GSE Smart IPTV)
  2. Add your BestIPTVToday subscription to the app
  3. On Roku: go to Settings → System → Screen mirroring → Prompt
  4. On Android: open Quick Settings → Cast → select your Roku
  5. Open your IPTV app on Android — it mirrors to your Roku/TV

Limitation: Screen mirroring uses your phone's battery and requires keeping the phone screen on. For a better Roku IPTV experience, consider Method 2 or upgrading to a Firestick.

Method 2 — Plex Media Server

Plex has an official Roku channel and can serve IPTV streams via a local media server:

  1. Install Plex on a Windows or Mac computer on your home network
  2. Use the Plex DVR feature or a compatible IPTV plugin to import your M3U playlist
  3. Install the Plex channel on Roku from the Roku Channel Store
  4. Open Plex on Roku — your IPTV channels appear in the Live TV section

Plex requires your computer to be on when streaming and may involve additional Plex Pass costs for DVR features.

Method 3 — Private Roku IPTV Channels

Some IPTV providers offer a dedicated private Roku channel (a channel accessible via a special code):

  1. Log in to my.roku.com on a computer
  2. Go to Manage Account → Add Private Channel
  3. Enter the private channel code (provided by your IPTV service)
  4. Add it and it appears on your Roku device after a refresh

Best Alternative: Switch to Firestick

Honestly, if IPTV is important to you, a Firestick 4K Max (~$60) is a far superior IPTV device than Roku. You get TiviMate, full Xtream Codes support, 4K HDR, and all IPTV apps without workarounds. Many users keep their Roku for casual streaming and add a Firestick stick specifically for IPTV.
