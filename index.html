Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know. Can't find an answer? Contact support anytime.

What is IPTV and how does it work?
IPTV (Internet Protocol Television) delivers TV content over the internet instead of traditional cable or satellite. You stream channels and on-demand content through an app using your existing internet connection. A stable connection of 10+ Mbps is recommended for the best experience.
How do I install and set up the service?
After purchasing, login credentials are sent to your email instantly. Download a compatible app (IPTV Smarters, TiviMate, or Perfect Player), enter your credentials, and start watching. We provide step-by-step guides for every device and our support team is always ready to help.
Is it compatible with my device?
Yes! Our service works on Samsung, LG, and Sony Smart TVs, Android phones and tablets, iPhones and iPads, Amazon Firestick, Roku, Apple TV, Windows and Mac computers, and MAG boxes. If it can access the internet, it can run BestIPTVToday.
What internet speed do I need?
For SD: 5 Mbps. For HD: 15 Mbps. For 4K Ultra HD: 25–30 Mbps. Most modern broadband connections handle this easily. A wired ethernet connection always delivers the most stable results vs. Wi-Fi.
Can I watch on multiple devices at once?
Yes! 1-month plan: 1 connection. 3-month: 2 connections. 6-month: 3 connections. 12-month: 5 simultaneous connections. Your whole family can watch different content on different devices at the same time.
Do you offer a free trial?
We offer a 24-hour test subscription at a minimal cost so you can verify quality and compatibility on your device before committing. Contact us on WhatsApp to request one. All paid plans also come with a 7-day satisfaction guarantee.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards, PayPal, cryptocurrency (Bitcoin, USDT, Ethereum), and bank transfers. All payments are processed through secure, encrypted gateways to protect your financial information.
Is there a contract or cancellation fee?
Absolutely not. No contracts, no hidden fees, no cancellation charges. Pay for your chosen period and simply don't renew if you decide to stop. We earn your loyalty through quality — not by trapping you in contracts.
Are you the beste IPTV Anbieter (best IPTV provider) for German-speaking users?
Yes! BestIPTVToday is widely recognised as the beste IPTV Anbieter for Germany, Austria and Switzerland. We offer 500+ German-language channels — ARD, ZDF, RTL, ProSieben, SAT.1, Sport1 and more — all in HD and 4K. German EPG (Programmführer), Catch-Up TV, and 24/7 German-language support are included in every plan.
Which apps work best with BestIPTVToday?
We are compatible with all major IPTV players: TiviMate (best for Android TV & Firestick), IPTV Smarters Pro (all platforms), Perfect Player, VLC, GSE Smart IPTV, SS IPTV, Smart IPTV, IBO Player, Televizo, and more. After subscribing you receive both M3U playlist URL and Xtream Codes credentials so any app works instantly.
